> “Fan Zhendong’s balance and low center of gravity are widely known; he is a phenomenal player. You can see his leg muscles; especially in the era of the larger ball, he was simply born for table tennis — he has everything a table tennis player needs.  
> His rally ability is outstanding; his serve and receive are excellent; he has no weaknesses.”
>
> — Jan-Ove Waldner
