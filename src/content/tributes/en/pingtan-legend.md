Fan Zhendong is an indelible mark in table tennis that cannot be hidden or bypassed. He makes many illustrious names seem ordinary. His career - the first half was spring breeze and peach blossoms, the second half was rivers in the night rain.

He is the sturdy pine in winter storms - heavy snow bends the pine, yet the pine stands tall and straight. He is the surging river thawing in early spring, breaking through the biting cold, winding into song across the awakening land. He is the beauty fans aspire to, a ray of sunshine in today's table tennis world. We fans chase the light and meet it, walk bathed in its glow.

He will only get better and better! He will become a legend of Chinese sports!
