At the Bundesliga championship venue, host Gu Xingyu said: "Fan Zhendong has truly stood on the European stage, and has shown the world the best of Chinese youth."

This quote reminds me of Fan Zhendong's fighting spirit - he's not just fighting for himself, but working hard for the image of the entire nation.

Team captain Franziska said in a post-match interview: "This season, we will win like this, because we have the best teammate in the world."
