When you keep climbing and stand at the peak of ten thousand mountains, looking back, you'll find that those who hated you are still in the same place, but now they can only look up at you.

Keep time for yourself, leave the rest to time. Right and wrong, warmth and coldness of human nature - time will eventually reward you.

— Wu Jingping's message on the day Fan Zhendong announced his withdrawal from world rankings
