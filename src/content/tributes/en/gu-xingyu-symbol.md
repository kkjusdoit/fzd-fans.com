Fan Zhendong has truly become a symbol of this era!

Friends watching in front of the screen, you may have already become parents, or will become parents. Perhaps one day you will tell them that there was a great athlete in this world. He taught us how to stay true to ourselves in good times, and how to swim against the current in adversity.

And Fan Zhendong has shown us that dreams can truly be more than just dreams. When you spare no effort to achieve them, looking back at the path you've traveled, it all becomes the most beautiful scenery along the way.

We are born human and will all grow old, but within this precious spiritual energy core, it will be passed down through generations.

Thank you, Fan Zhendong!
