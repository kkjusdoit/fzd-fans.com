# High EQ Quotes

## 1. Post-Match Interview After Mixed Doubles Victory with Ding Ning

Reporter: Which shot did Ding Ning play beautifully?

> "Every single one"

Reporter: Before, did you see Ding Ning as a big sister, a Grand Slam champion? Now (after partnering), do you feel differently?

> "First of all, I never thought of her as a big sister, only as a Grand Slam champion"

This made Ding Ning burst into a big smile.

## 2. About His Biggest Rival

When asked who his biggest rival is:

> "If I say it's myself, that sounds a bit too cliché. I think future rivals could emerge at any time"

## 3. Post-Match Interview After Mixed Doubles Victory with Chen Meng

When asked about expectations for the World Championships:

> "My expectation is the same as this World Championships - keep winning"

Chen Meng couldn't stop laughing. While she was still organizing her thoughts saying "My expectation is definitely the final...", Fan Zhendong interjected:

> "Your expectation is my expectation"

## 4. Greater Bay Area Gala (Part 1)

When the host "challenged" him to evaluate whose Cantonese was most standard among Ma Li and others:

> "Mine is the most standard"

## 5. Greater Bay Area Gala (Part 2)

Host Lan Yu asked: Which performance are you most looking forward to?

> "Why is it always this kind of question? I'm looking forward to every performance"

Lan Yu was prepared: Why is it always this kind of answer?

Fan Zhendong finally said:

> "I'm more looking forward to my own performance later"

Comment: "Lan Yu is clever, but Fan Zhendong is wise"
