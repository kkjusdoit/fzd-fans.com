# Classic Memes Collection

## Egg Pancake (Jidan Guanbing)

Fan Zhendong's joke about coaches requiring 10,000-meter runs during training.

> "When the coach says 10,000 meters, it sounds as easy as me eating an egg pancake"

## "Bring a Pancake"

On March 11, 2012 at 7:39 AM, Kong Lingxuan posted on Weibo "How great it would be if this were true", and Fan Zhendong commented at 8:39 AM:

> "Bring a pancake"

## "I Don't Play This Game"

During an early livestream, when asked: "Who's your favorite on the men's team?"

> "Me, I don't play this game"
> "I said I don't play this game"

## "Saying I Sing Better Than Zhou Yu, Isn't That an Insult?"

A classic high-EQ response during an early livestream.

## "What Teenager Wears Dress Shirts?"

During a room interview, discussing team members' clothing styles and joking about Ma Long's many dress shirts. Fan Zhendong said:

> "What teenager wears dress shirts?"

## Got So Mad Losing to Wang Hao

In 2013, playing against Wang Hao, he was reverse-swept after having a big lead, making 16-year-old Fan Zhendong visibly angry. His frustrated expression made Liu Guoliang, Wang Liqin and others laugh, and Coach Wu went down to comfort him.

## Never Get Up

Famous English phrase, "Never get up" (a play on "Never give up").

When the mainland Olympic delegation visited Hong Kong and Macau, Pan Zhanle not only asked reporters if they knew about Fan Zhendong's "Never give up" meme, but also added "Never get up" to his social media bio.

> "Never give up" → "Never get up"
