## Caring for Youth: Igniting Hope Through Sports

Recently, Olympic table tennis champion Fan Zhendong appeared at the headquarters of the United Nations High Commissioner for Refugees (UNHCR) in Geneva, Switzerland, attending the "Moving for Change" humanitarian exchange event and speaking on site. He called on all sectors of society to pay greater attention to displaced youth, using the power of sports to heal trauma, ignite hope, and help children regain confidence and courage amidst uncertainty and adversity.

At the event, Fan Zhendong drew upon his personal athletic journey to share his understanding of the value of sports. He noted that as a professional athlete, sports has given him far more than competitive experiences on the court; it has instilled the confidence and courage to confront challenges, as well as the strength found in persevering shoulder-to-shoulder with others. For children forced to flee their homes, having access to sporting activities means they can experience the pure joy of movement alongside peers. He hopes children will discover smiles and confidence through sports, believing in themselves—because "everyone can become their own champion."

During the visit, Fan Zhendong also published an essay on his social media accounts reflecting on his impressions of Geneva. He wrote that witnessing the launch of the new three-year cycle of the "Moving for Change" initiative in Geneva deepened his appreciation of the significance of sports. For children displaced by conflict and hardship, a single piece of sporting equipment may seem modest, but it can serve as a vital starting point for reclaiming life, integrating into communities, and sustaining courage. Sports might not immediately transform their immediate physical environment, but it empowers them to feel connection, happiness, and hope amid profound misfortune and adversity.

Fan Zhendong emphasized his enduring belief that sports is never merely a competitive arena reserved for a select few; rather, it should serve as a universal language from which everyone can draw strength. Sports transcends barriers of language, borders, and culture, uniting people and offering emotional outlets and developmental support for youth facing adversity. True philanthropy extends beyond fleeting goodwill—it lies in translating goodwill into sustained, long-term action. Expressing deep honor at taking part in this humanitarian effort, Fan noted that an athlete's platform carries responsibility. He looks forward to seeing more people champion displaced youth, making sports an accessible daily reality for more children worldwide.

From competitive courts to international humanitarian frontlines, Fan Zhendong's Geneva journey represents both the transmission of the sporting spirit and a resonant call for responsibility and compassion. While sports might not immediately change external realities, it plants seeds of hope within young hearts, accompanying them as they walk toward a wider and brighter future.
