## Fan Zhendong's Complete Triumph! Back-to-Back 3-0 Sweeps in Bundesliga

On the evening of September 5, 2026, the 1,100 tickets at the ARAG CenterCourt in Düsseldorf were completely sold out. Every spectator was watching one name — **Fan Zhendong**.

Exactly a year earlier, on August 31, 2025, Fan made his Bundesliga debut representing FC Saarbrücken against TTC Schwalbe Bergneustadt. That evening, he fell 2-3 to Romain Ruiz and 1-3 to Benedikt Duda, dropping two individual points as Saarbrücken lost 1-3.

On September 5, the script was completely rewritten. Donning the jersey of Borussia Düsseldorf against the very same club and the exact two opponents, Fan was utterly untouchable:

- **Match 1**: Fan Zhendong **3:0** Romain Ruiz (11:7, 11:7, 11:8)
- **Match 4**: Fan Zhendong **3:0** Benedikt Duda (11:8, 11:8, 11:8)

Two singles matches, six games played, zero games lost. Borussia Düsseldorf triumphed 3-1 over Bergneustadt to maintain a flawless 4-0 record at the top of the Bundesliga table.

---

### Match 1: Neutralizing the Unorthodox "Strawberry Flick" (3-0 vs Ruiz)

France's Romain Ruiz is renowned for his unorthodox style, especially his treacherous reverse-banana ("strawberry") flick that creates unpredictable angles and spins. In 2025, Ruiz dragged Fan into a grueling five-game battle to pull off a 3-2 upset.

One year later, Fan came prepared. Ruiz's unique flicks failed to faze him. With meticulous short-table control, Fan gave his opponent no opening. The scores read 11:7, 11:7, 11:8 as Fan pulled away midway through each game to seal a clinical 3-0 win.

Head coach Danny Heister remarked: *"Fan Zhendong had lost to both Romain Ruiz and Benedikt Duda last season, but his training condition has been phenomenal. We prepared intensively for this match, and he delivered two crucial points here."*

---

### Match 2 & 3: Källberg's Heartbreak & Dang Qiu's Dominance

- **Match 2**: Anton Källberg faced Benedikt Duda. After dropping game one 7:11, Källberg rallied to take games two and three (11:6, 12:10). In the deciding fifth game, Källberg led 10:7 with multiple match points, but Duda staged a remarkable comeback to snatch the decider 13:11. The team score stood at 1-1.
- **Match 3**: European Champion Dang Qiu faced Belgium's Adrien Rassenfosse. Qiu dominated in straight games (11:4, 11:5, 11:2), conceding just 11 points across three games to restore Düsseldorf's lead at 2-1.

---

### Match 4: Tactical Mastery and Three Identical 11-8s (3-0 vs Duda)

The fourth match pitted two titans against each other. Duda, Germany's national team star and top-ranked player of the previous Bundesliga season (25 wins, 8 losses), had defeated Fan 3-1 last year.

This time, Fan's tactical execution was flawless: pinpoint serve placement to disrupt Duda's aggressive return-of-serve attacks, neutralizing Duda's formidable backhand firepower, and dictating multi-ball rallies.

- Game 1: Tied at 8-8, Fan locked down crucial points: **11:8**.
- Game 2: Duda attempted all-out aggression; Fan absorbed the pressure: **11:8**.
- Game 3: In a high-stakes finale, Fan broke away after 8-8: **11:8**.

Three identical 11:8 scorelines — not a coincidence, but an exhibition of total control.

---

### A Clean Slate

When the final ball dropped, ARAG CenterCourt erupted in celebration. Head coach Danny Heister and teammates rose for a standing ovation, and traveling fans chanted Fan's name.

Borussia Düsseldorf extended their perfect league start to four consecutive wins (12-2 match score), sitting comfortably atop the standings. Fan Zhendong settled the one-year-old score in the most emphatic way possible — with two masterclass 3-0 sweeps.
