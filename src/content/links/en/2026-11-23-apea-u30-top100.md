### Honor Archive: APEA 2026 Dual Rankings Selection

> **Lead**: In the 2026 annual rankings released by the APEA Leaders' Initiative (Asia-Pacific Entrepreneurs Annual Forum), Chinese table tennis Olympic champion **Fan Zhendong** was simultaneously named to both the **Asia-Pacific U30 Outstanding Young Leaders** list and the **2026 Asia-Pacific Top 100 Most Influential Young Men** list—making him the only table tennis player to earn spots on both lists.

---

## Key Highlights

1. **Second Selection as "Asia-Pacific U30 Outstanding Young Leader"**  
   Covering young leaders aged 30 and under across all 21 APEC member economies, the U30 list evaluates professional excellence, social responsibility, and regional influence. This marks Fan Zhendong's second time receiving the honor.

2. **Named to "2026 Asia-Pacific Top 100 Most Influential Young Men"**  
   Bringing together standout male figures across sports, technology, business, and culture in the Asia-Pacific region, the Top 100 ranking recognizes Fan Zhendong's enduring athletic stature and cross-disciplinary reach.

3. **Impact On and Beyond the Court**  
   From winning men's singles and team gold at the Paris Olympics to claiming a European treble in the German Bundesliga and visiting UNHCR headquarters in Geneva to advocate for displaced youth through sports, Fan Zhendong continues to elevate table tennis onto a broader global stage.
