# What His Name Means

Chinese names carry deep meaning. Let's decode "Zhendong" (振东).

## Character Breakdown

### 振 (Zhèn)
- **Meaning:** To rise, to inspire, to energize
- **Usage:** 振奋 (zhènfèn) = to inspire/invigorate
- **Connotation:** Strength, revival, uplifting energy

### 东 (Dōng)
- **Meaning:** East, sunrise, new beginnings
- **Usage:** 东方 (dōngfāng) = the East
- **Connotation:** Hope, dawn, the rising sun

## Combined Meaning

Together, **"Zhendong" (振东)** can be interpreted as:

> "Revitalizing the East" — a name full of strength and hope.

It carries the weight of rising suns and boundless potential. A fitting name for someone who has indeed revitalized Chinese table tennis on the world stage.

## The Family Name

**樊 (Fán)** is a relatively uncommon Chinese surname. It has historical roots dating back to ancient China and is associated with nobility and scholarly traditions.
