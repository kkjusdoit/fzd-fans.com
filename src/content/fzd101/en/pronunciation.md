# How to Pronounce "Fan Zhendong"

Many international fans are unsure how to pronounce "Fan Zhendong". Here's your complete guide!

## The Name in Chinese

**樊振东** (Fán Zhèndōng)

## Syllable Breakdown

| Character | Pinyin | IPA | Sounds Like |
|-----------|--------|-----|-------------|
| 樊 | Fán | /fɑn/ | Like "fun" but with "ah" |
| 振 | Zhèn | /ʈʂən/ | Like "jun" with a "zh" sound |
| 东 | Dōng | /tʊŋ/ | Like "dong" in "ding-dong" |

## Full IPA Transcription

`/fɑn ʈʂən tʊŋ/`

## Cultural Note

In Chinese culture, the family name comes first. So "Fan Zhendong" means "Zhendong of the Fan family". 

The family name **Fan (樊)** is relatively rare in China, making it quite distinctive.
