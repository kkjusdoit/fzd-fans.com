# Nicknames Fans Use

In Chinese fan culture, nicknames often express closeness rather than ridicule. Here are the most beloved ones:

## 小胖 (Xiǎo Pàng) - "Little Fatty"

### The "Xiao Pang" Dilemma

⚠️ **Important Cultural Context:** This might sound offensive in English, but it's actually a term of endearment!

In Chinese culture, calling someone "Little Fatty" is affectionate, not body shaming. It represents:

- Being cute and cherubic
- Looking well-fed and healthy  
- His adorable round face when he was younger

**It's how fans express their love for his cute appearance!** 🥰

---

## 东哥 (Dōng Gē) - "Brother Dong"

A respectful yet familiar way to address him, showing admiration while maintaining closeness. "Ge" (哥) means "older brother" and is commonly used to show respect to male figures you admire.

---

## The Violence Panda 🐼💥

**Origin:** Coined by international commentators

**Why:** "He looks cuddly like a panda, but plays with ferocious power."

*Deceptively cute, incredibly dangerous.*

This nickname perfectly captures the contrast between his gentle appearance and explosive playing style.

---

## 铁血一单 (Tiě Xuè Yī Dān) - "Iron-Blooded No.1 Singles"

Refers to his unwavering determination as China's top singles player for over a decade. "Iron-blooded" (铁血) conveys:

- Unyielding spirit
- Warrior-like determination
- Never backing down under pressure

- **中国长城 (Zhōngguó Chángchéng)** - "The Wall of China" - For his impenetrable defense
