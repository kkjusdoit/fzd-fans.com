# The Personality Gap

> On the table: explosive power. Off the table: calm and introverted.

## Stats vs. Vibes

### 🔥 On Court

**Keywords:** Ferocious, Speed, Power, The Wall

- Explosive forehand loops
- Relentless attacks
- Impenetrable defense
- Intense focus and determination
- The famous "death stare" during crucial points

### ☁️ Off Court

**Keywords:** Gentle, Shy, Foodie, Sleepy

- Quiet but sharp-minded
- Emotionally restrained in public
- Highly self-disciplined
- Loves good food (especially hotpot!)
- Needs his beauty sleep

## Key Personality Traits

1. **🧠 Quiet but sharp-minded** - Don't let his silence fool you; he's always thinking
2. **😶 Emotionally restrained** - Rarely shows extreme emotions in public
3. **💪 Highly self-disciplined** - His training regimen is legendary
4. **🍜 Secret foodie** - Has a well-known love for hotpot and good food
5. **😴 Sleep enthusiast** - Values his rest and recovery time

## The Contrast That Fans Love

This "gap moe" (反差萌) - the cute contrast between his fierce on-court persona and gentle off-court personality - is one of the reasons fans adore him so much.

He's proof that you can be both:
- A fierce competitor AND a gentle soul
- Intensely focused AND laid-back
- A world champion AND humble
