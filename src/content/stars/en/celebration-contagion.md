## Symbol Spreader

Fan Zhendong has constructed a universal emotional language that transcends borders and sports by adopting classic celebration moves from European football stars (such as Mbappé, Bellingham, and Ronaldo).

This language has been quickly decoded and replicated by his competitors, teammates, and fan communities, forming a collective celebration based on "admiration psychology" and "emotional resonance."
