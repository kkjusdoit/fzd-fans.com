## I. Origin of the Letters
Fan Zhendong previously did not have any personal social media accounts. Since 2021, he communicated with fans through the fan club's Weibo (which was posted using a Huawei Mate40 Porsche Design phone). Because the posting device contained the word "Porsche", the four long letters were affectionately called <strong>"Porsche Family Letters"</strong> by fans.

## II. Core Advocacies
* **🚫 Reject Fandom Culture**: Opposes airport pick-ups, fan club fundraising, voting campaigns, and following his personal life. Stresses that the athlete-fan relationship belongs exclusively to the sports arena.
* **🏠 Protect Personal Space**: Except for official team schedules, personal schedules should not be intruded. He only accepts handwritten letters and refuses gifts to avoid fan economy monetization.
* **💡 Call for Rational Support**: Urges fans to focus on their own lives, support him through match performances, and resist privacy invasion or online toxicity.
