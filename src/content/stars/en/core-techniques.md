## Tyrant Flip / Violent Flip
Directly powering through short balls with flip attacks, his signature unreasonable technique. In positions where conventional play would push or flick lightly, Fan Zhendong chooses to violently flip, catching opponents off guard.

## Backhand Quick Rip
Ultra-fast attacks from the backhand side, with great speed and angles. Fan Zhendong's backhand quick rip is not only amazingly fast but also precisely placed, often ending rallies before opponents can react.

## Fierce Line Changes
Extremely skilled at changing to straight lines from both forehand and backhand. When opponents expect cross-court shots, Fan Zhendong always surprises with straight-line winners, giving him huge advantages in rallies.

## Forehand Power Loop
Powerful forehand attacks, you can even hear the explosive sound of contact. Fan Zhendong's forehand power loops are not only incredibly powerful but also heavily spun, with contact sounds louder than anyone else's.

## Mid-to-Far Table Counter-Looping
Extremely deep rally capabilities, maintaining high-quality counterattacks even when pushed back. Even when forced to mid-to-far table positions, Fan Zhendong can maintain high-quality attacks, enabling comebacks from passive situations.

## Lightning-Fast Transitions
T0-level forehand-backhand transition speed, no gaps. Fan Zhendong's forehand-backhand transitions are near-perfect, making it extremely difficult for opponents to find openings during his switches.

## No Dead Zones
Balanced forehand and backhand, no obvious weaknesses. No matter where the ball lands on the table, Fan Zhendong can return high-quality shots, leaving opponents with nowhere to attack.
